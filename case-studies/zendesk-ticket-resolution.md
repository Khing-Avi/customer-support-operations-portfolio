# Zendesk Ticket Resolution Case Study

## Problem

while I was working at SPECTRA, there was a situation where A Customer reported repeated payment failure while attempting to complete a subscription purchase.

## Investigation

I Reviewed customer account records and verified payment logs.

## Tools Used

Zendesk  
Google Sheets  
Internal CRM

## Resolution

Identified a payment gateway timeout issue and escalated to the technical team.

## Result

Issue resolved and customer was able to complete the transaction successfully.
