## CRM Data Cleanup & Data Entry Accuracy Project
## Objective

To demonstrate accurate data entry and data cleanup processes within a customer relationship management system.

## Problem
Over time, CRM systems may accumulate:

- duplicate records
- incomplete fields
- outdated contact information
These issues reduce operational efficiency and reporting accuracy.

## Process
1. Export customer records from CRM database.
2. Identify duplicate entries using spreadsheet filters and sorting tools.
3. Verify customer information across available data sources.
4. Standardize data formats.

Examples:
- Phone numbers
- Customer names
- Email addresses

5. Update and re-import corrected records into CRM system.

## Tools Used

- Microsoft Excel
- Google Sheets
- CRM database tools

## Quality Control Measures

double verification of updated records
cross-checking email and contact fields
structured data formatting

## Outcome

- Improved data quality ensures:
- more reliable customer records
- accurate reporting
- improved customer communication.
