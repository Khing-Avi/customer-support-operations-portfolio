## Zendesk Ticket Management Workflow
## Objective

To demonstrate a structured workflow for managing customer support tickets efficiently while maintaining service quality and response time targets.

## Tools Used
Zendesk
Google Sheets
Email support systems
Internal CRM tools

## Workflow Process

## 1. Ticket Intake
Customer requests are received through support channels including email and help desk forms. Each request automatically generates a support ticket.

## 2. Ticket Categorization
Tickets are categorized based on issue type:

Payment issue
Account access
Technical issue
General inquiry

## Priority Assignment
Tickets are prioritized according to urgency.

## Example
| Priority | Response Time   |
| -------- | --------------- |
| High     | Immediate       |
| Medium   | Within 2 hours  |
| Low      | Within 24 hours |


## Investigation
Agent reviews account information, transaction logs, and previous interactions to identify the root cause.

## Customer Communication
Clear response sent to the customer explaining the issue and resolution steps.

## Resolution and Closure
Once the issue is resolved, the ticket is closed and customer satisfaction feedback may be requested.

## Result
This structured workflow helps maintain:
faster response times

improved customer satisfaction

organized ticket management
